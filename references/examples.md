## Example 1: Technology Assessment (Perovskite Solar Cells)

### 青龙 Seeds
1. Efficiency race: 26.1% lab record, approaching silicon
2. Stability paradox: degrades in moisture, heat, light — the very things solar panels face
3. Manufacturing cost: solution-processable = potentially 1/10 of silicon fab cost
4. Lead toxicity: most efficient perovskites contain Pb — regulatory risk
5. Space application: 25 W/g specific power + radiation self-healing → ideal for satellites

- 🟡 "Approaching silicon" ignores that silicon is a moving target (HJT, TOPCon)
- 🟢 Space application is robust — no moisture/heat concerns, specific power advantage is physics-based

### 玄武 Convergence
- Confidence 0.85: Perovskite will find niche markets (space, BIPV) before competing with silicon at scale
- Confidence 0.40: Perovskite will replace silicon as dominant PV within 15 years
- Residual: tandem (perovskite-on-silicon) economics not yet clear

### 土 Reality Check
- Verification: Check Oxford PV's commercial tandem module output (expected 2026)
- Next action: Talk to one BIPV installer about willingness to try perovskite
- Failure signal: If Oxford PV delays again, tandem thesis weakens significantly

### First Principles
Solar energy conversion is fundamentally a bandgap optimization problem. The optimal bandgap for single-junction terrestrial PV is ~1.34 eV (Shockley-Queisser). Perovskite's tunability (1.2-2.3 eV) makes it uniquely suited for tandem architectures — the question is not "perovskite vs silicon" but "what's the optimal bandgap stack?"

## Example 2: Compressed Mode (Quick Strategy Question)

**Q: Should a traditional electrical installation company pivot to AI computing centers?**

🌿 Seeds: (1) 50-55% of compute center costs overlap with existing EPC capabilities, (2) daughter has AI background — generational bridge, (3) real estate dependency is terminal risk

🔥 Core: Three progressive paths — A: Pure EPC contracting (low risk, 15-20% margin), B: JV with compute operator (recommended, shared risk/upside), C: Own brand (high risk, needs ¥50M+)

⚔️ Attack: "Capability overlap" is misleading — electrical installation ≠ high-density power distribution for GPU racks. Cooling expertise gap is critical. Path C without domain expertise = expensive lesson.

💧 Verdict: Path B (JV) at 0.75 confidence. Let daughter build the AI side; leverage parent company's construction + grid connections.

🌍 Reality: Visit 3 operating compute centers this month. Talk to their EPC contractors. If they say "we'd love a partner who handles power + cooling," thesis confirmed.
